01About

I am Raman Sharma, an Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) graduate from UIET, Kurukshetra University, with a strong interest in electronics, embedded systems, semiconductor technology, and emerging technologies.

I have gained practical industry experience as a Testing Engineer at Dixon Technologies (Padget Electronics), working in the assembly-line testing environment for Motorola mobile devices — troubleshooting and resolving issues across systems such as UCT, FOD, Vision, AR, and DepthVAL, with hands-on exposure to electronics manufacturing, testing, debugging, and production processes.

My academic and technical journey also includes practical exposure to Cadence tools during my internship at SCL Mohali, and an ISRO internship focused on packaging process flow and CA-level finalisation of 100-pin CQFZ and 68-pin CQFJ packages.

Alongside my professional work, I'm interested in research and academic development — Embedded Systems, VLSI, semiconductor technology, wireless sensor networks, machine learning, and privacy-preserving technologies — and I maintain technical work and publications through my online repositories.
